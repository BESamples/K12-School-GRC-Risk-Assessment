# Project Overview

## Project Name

Northstar High School Cybersecurity Risk and Control Assessment

## Organization

Northstar High School is a fictional public high school used for this simulated GRC assessment.

The school has approximately:

* 800 students
* 70 teachers
* 20 administrative and support employees
* 5 members of the information technology team

The school uses a Windows-based network to manage student records, employee information, shared files, administrative documents, and classroom technology.

## Business Need

Northstar High School depends on technology to support classroom instruction, student services, administrative operations, and communication.

The school stores information that may include:

* Student names and identification numbers
* Grades and attendance records
* Disciplinary records
* Student health information
* Parent and guardian contact information
* Employee records
* Payroll-related information
* Administrative documents

Unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure of this information could disrupt school operations and harm students, employees, and the school district.

The school has requested a simulated cybersecurity assessment to determine whether appropriate security controls are in place.

## Assessment Purpose

The purpose of this project is to evaluate the security controls protecting Northstar High School’s simulated Windows environment and sensitive information.

The assessment will identify control weaknesses, evaluate cybersecurity risks, collect evidence, and recommend corrective actions.

## Assessment Objectives

The assessment will:

1. Identify important systems and information assets.
2. Determine which assets require additional protection.
3. Identify cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities.
4. Evaluate the likelihood and impact of identified risks.
5. Review selected administrative and technical security controls.
6. Test controls using evidence from the simulated lab.
7. Document security findings.
8. Recommend risk-treatment actions.
9. Create a remediation tracking plan.
10. Provide an executive summary for school leadership.

## Assessment Approach

The assessment will combine documentation review, technical testing, observation, and simulated interviews.

Technical evidence may include:

* Wazuh alerts
* Windows event logs
* Sysmon events
* File Integrity Monitoring alerts
* Active Directory screenshots
* PowerShell logs
* YARA detection results
* Group Policy settings
* Vulnerability scan results
* Incident-response records

## Project Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* All in-scope systems are documented.
* Major information assets are classified.
* At least ten cybersecurity risks are evaluated.
* At least ten security controls are tested.
* Supporting evidence is collected for each tested control.
* Findings include clear risks and recommendations.
* Corrective actions are entered into a Plan of Action and Milestones.
* A final assessment report is completed.

## Limitations

This project is performed in a simulated lab environment.

The assessment does not include:

* Real student information
* Real employee information
* Production school systems
* Physical penetration testing
* Destructive security testing
* Legal conclusions regarding compliance

The results represent the condition of the simulated environment at the time testing is performed.
